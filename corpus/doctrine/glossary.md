# MDMP Planning Glossary

## Course of Action (COA)

A broad potential solution that accomplishes the mission. Each COA includes a scheme of maneuver, main effort, supporting efforts, and key tasks sufficient for war gaming and comparison.

## Commander's intent

A concise expression of the purpose of the operation and the desired military end state. It guides initiative when the plan no longer fits the situation.

## War game

See Step 4 — visualization of a COA through critical events; not the same as COA comparison.

## Decision matrix

A tabular comparison of COAs against evaluation criteria, used in Step 5 to support the commander's decision.

## OPORD

Operations order — the formal order issued after COA approval (Step 7: Orders Production).

## CCIR

Commander's critical information requirement — information the commander needs for timely decisions.

## Running estimate

Continuous assessment of facts and assumptions affecting the mission; updated throughout MDMP.

## Main effort

The unit or axis assigned the decisive task; receives priority of support.

## Synchronization matrix

A tool to align friendly actions, reactions, and counteractions across time and space during war gaming.

## Mission analysis

MDMP Step 2 — understanding the problem, defining tasks, developing criteria, and drafting intent.

## COA comparison

MDMP Step 5 — systematic comparison of COAs using evaluation criteria; follows war gaming.
