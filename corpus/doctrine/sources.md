# Doctrine sources

This corpus uses **summarized** public military planning concepts. For authoritative text, consult official publications:

- **FM 5-0** — *Planning and Orders Production* (Army Publishing Directorate; current edition Nov 2024)
- **ADP 5-0** — *The Operations Process* (Army doctrine primer; Jul 2019)
- **CALL Handbook 15-06** — *Military Decisionmaking Process*

Do not treat this repository as a replacement for official doctrine. When in doubt, cite the publication, not this training corpus.
