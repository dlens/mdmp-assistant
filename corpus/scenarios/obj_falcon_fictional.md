# Fictional Scenario — OBJ FALCON River Crossing

**Training fiction only.** No real units, customers, or operations.

## Situation

**Unit:** 3rd Brigade Combat Team (3rd BCT), 11th Infantry Division (fictional)

**Mission:** Seize **OBJ FALCON** — a river crossing and town center — within **36 hours** to restore freedom of movement.

**Enemy:** Reinforced enemy battalion with prepared positions on the far bank and indirect fire in range.

**Planning level:** Brigade deliberate attack.

## Evaluation criteria (four selected)

| Criterion | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Mission Accomplishment | Likelihood of seizing OBJ FALCON within time |
| Speed to Objective | Time to secure the decisive terrain |
| Enemy Disruption | Degree of disruption to enemy defense |
| Simplicity | C2 burden, synchronization, subordinate initiative |

## Courses of action (five)

| COA | Scheme |
|-----|--------|
| COA-A Direct Assault | Main effort on Highway 12 axis; supporting attack fixes forward |
| COA-B Double Envelopment | Fix north, main effort flanks south to seize crossing from rear |
| COA-C Air Assault Link-up | Air assault seizes far bank; ground link-up within 24 hours |
| COA-D Feint and Main | Demonstration north; main attack south with deception |
| COA-E Turning Movement | Bypass OBJ; seize enemy supply hub to force withdrawal |

## Coaching notes (for training pairs)

- **COA-A** tends toward higher simplicity, moderate speed
- **COA-B** strong mission and disruption potential, lower simplicity
- **COA-C** highest speed if air assault succeeds, highest synchronization risk
- **COA-D** good disruption via deception, moderate simplicity
- **COA-E** strong disruption, weaker direct mission accomplishment against OBJ FALCON

Staff assess these in Step 4 (war game), then compare in Step 5 using the decision matrix — not during the war game itself.
